# Life tables

`who2019.yaml` is generated by `go run ./tools/genlifetables` (or
`make lifetables`) from public WHO APIs; do not edit it by hand.

* `q[i]` — probability of dying between exact ages 18+i and 19+i, obtained
  from WHO five-year `nqx` by interpolating the log force of mortality
  between band midpoints. The recomputed life expectancy at 20 matches WHO's
  published e(20) within 0.03 years for every table.
* `ex[i]` — remaining life expectancy at exact age 18+i, recomputed from the
  single-year table and WHO's e(85).
* `bands[].fractions` — share of deaths per five-year band in six groups,
  from WHO Global Health Estimates (year 2019): cardiovascular (GHE 1100 +
  diabetes 800), oncology (610), liver (digestive 1210 + alcohol 860 + drug
  use 870 + poisonings 1540), trauma (injuries 1510 minus poisonings and
  self-harm), self_harm (1610), other (the rest). "World" sums every country.

Chance of living from 18 to 85 per table: world male 27.6%, world female
41.7%, Russia male 15.7%, Russia female 38.2%, USA male 37.5%, USA female 50.5%.
